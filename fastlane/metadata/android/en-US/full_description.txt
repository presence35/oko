See what's coming — and how close it is.

Око is a civilian air-threat map for Ukraine. Official alerts tell you a whole oblast is under attack. Око shows you the actual picture: where the threats are, which way they're heading, and how close they'll get — and it tells you, based on YOUR own zones.

YOUR OWN WARNING ZONES
Set a red and a yellow ring around wherever you are. Get a red-zone siren, a quieter yellow nudge, or nothing at all outside them. You choose the distance, not the app.

FAST AND SLOW, SEPARATELY
Ballistic missiles and Shahed-type drones behave nothing alike. Set separate radii, timing and alerts for fast and slow threats.

ALERT BY TYPE
Some threats matter to you, some don't. Turn alerts on or off per threat type, and decide what appears on the map.

PER-ZONE SWITCHES
Arm only the rings you want. Day and night have separate values, so your settings can change with the time of day.

YOUR FREQUENCY, YOUR CALL
Every change, once per threat, once per type, or a digest with a window you set. No more being woken for things you already know.

NIGHT MODE THAT ACTUALLY SLEEPS
One tap silences everything until morning — then restores exactly what you had. Or it alerts you more intensely if that's what you choose.

THE FULL PICTURE
Live tracks with predicted headings, distance and ETA, official oblast and raion alerts, a home-screen widget, and the nearest shelters with walking times.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No tracking. Your location is processed on your phone and never uploaded.

Око is an independent civilian tool, not an official alert source — always follow official air-raid signals. Positions and distances are approximate.
